# Viandly 0.9.0-beta.21

Released: August 1, 2026

This beta update makes administrator responsibility transferable through the product and makes
private recipe links useful after their creation dialog has been closed.

## Highlights

- Administrators can grant administrator access to another active account and revoke access from
  another administrator through Account Administration.
- Role transfers are audited, require confirmation, cannot target the acting administrator, and
  cannot remove the final active administrator.
- Active private recipe links can be reopened and sent again through Copy, Email, the device share
  action, or a ready-to-send copied message.
- Existing private links remain valid. Owners can replace older links whose original URLs cannot be
  recovered; replacement immediately stops the former URL.
- Sharing Help now explains active-link reuse, revocation, and legacy-link replacement.

## Tester Guidance

- Create a private recipe link, close the sharing dialog, reopen it, select **Show link**, and verify
  that Copy, Email, Share, and Copy message are available again.
- An older link should be labeled as such and offer **Replace link**. Confirm replacement only when
  invalidating the old URL is acceptable.
- Administrators can test a role transfer with a separate active account. Self-demotion and removal
  of the final active administrator must remain blocked.

## Release Identity

The product version is `0.9.0-beta.21` and the source tag is `v0.9.0-beta.21`. The About and Help
interfaces show the exact 12-character source revision used by the production build.

## Compatibility

This release adds one nullable field to private recipe share records. Existing links and recipes are
not rewritten or invalidated. Existing authentication methods, account entitlements, recipes,
images, imports, cookbooks, and archives remain compatible.
