# Viandly 0.9.0-beta.24

Released: August 5, 2026

This beta update improves Google account selection and sign-in feedback, replaces native browser
confirmation prompts with consistent Viandly dialogs, and standardizes spacing within action
buttons throughout the application.

## Highlights

- **Continue with Google** now asks Google to display its account chooser instead of silently
  reusing the browser's current Google identity.
- Google sign-in now provides useful guidance when an invitation is required, expired, or otherwise
  unavailable, instead of presenting a generic server-configuration error.
- Administrator assignment, account suspension and reactivation, session revocation, and permanent
  recipe deletion now use clear Viandly confirmation dialogs.
- Permanent recipe deletion identifies the affected recipe and explains that it cannot be restored.
- Buttons now consistently separate icons, labels, and counters across home, recipe, settings,
  import, cookbook, shopping, and administrative pages.

## Tester Guidance

- Sign out, choose **Continue with Google**, and confirm that Google displays an account chooser.
- Try an uninvited Google address and confirm that Viandly explains the beta invitation requirement.
- Review administrator account actions and permanent recipe deletion to confirm that Viandly
  dialogs replace browser confirmation popups.
- Check icon-and-label buttons on the signed-in home page and other work areas for consistent
  spacing.

## Release Identity

The product version is `0.9.0-beta.24` and the source tag is `v0.9.0-beta.24`. The About and Help
interfaces show the exact 12-character source revision used by the production build.

## Compatibility

This release does not change the database schema or rewrite existing recipe, account, import,
image, cookbook, billing, or AI data.
