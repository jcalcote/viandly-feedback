# Viandly 0.9.0-beta.26

Released: August 22, 2026

This beta update makes shopping lists practical for multi-store trips. You can maintain your usual
store route, assign ingredients to stores, and adjust the route for a specific shopping trip
without changing your account default.

## Highlights

- Shopping now includes a dedicated **Manage stores** page for maintaining and ordering your usual
  stores.
- Store names save automatically while you edit them.
- Shopping-list items can be assigned or moved to a store through a compact contextual menu.
- Needed items are grouped under store headings, with unassigned items kept in a clear final
  section.
- Store sections can be reordered for a particular trip and reset to your account's default route.
- Marking an item handled retains its store assignment, making it easy to correct or reopen an item
  while shopping.
- Deleting a store preserves its shopping items and returns them to **Not assigned**.
- Viandly account archives now preserve store catalogs, item assignments, and per-list ordering.
- Shopping Help documents store setup, assignment, route changes, and deletion behavior.

## Tester Guidance

- Open **Shopping**, choose **Manage stores**, add several stores, and arrange them in the order you
  normally visit them.
- Open a shopping list and assign needed items to different stores. Confirm that each item moves
  into the corresponding store section.
- Reorder populated store sections for the current trip, then choose **Use default store order**.
- Mark an assigned item handled and reopen it. Confirm that its store assignment is retained.
- Rename a store and confirm that the name saves automatically and updates existing lists.
- Delete a store and confirm that its items move to **Not assigned** without being deleted.

## Release Identity

The product version is `0.9.0-beta.26` and the source tag is `v0.9.0-beta.26`. The About and Help
interfaces show the exact 12-character source revision used by the production build.

## Compatibility

This release includes an additive database migration. Existing shopping lists and items are
preserved; existing items begin without a store assignment. Older Viandly archives without store
data remain importable, while new archives carry the additional shopping-store data.
