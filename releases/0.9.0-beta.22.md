# Viandly 0.9.0-beta.22

Released: August 3, 2026

This beta update makes Viandly's AI features practical across three major services, makes initial AI
setup understandable without provider jargon, and makes it easier to return to recipes and manage
Inbox work.

## Highlights

- Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude now join OpenAI as complete Viandly AI service choices for
  recipe import and repair, recipe development, cookbook design, and Cook Assist.
- Cook Assist uses native realtime voice with OpenAI and Gemini. Claude supplies cooking reasoning
  while compatible browser speech services provide listening and spoken playback.
- AI Assistant settings now compare services in plain language, link directly to each service's key
  page, explain how to create and safely retain an access key, and distinguish consumer
  subscriptions from API access.
- Viandly retrieves the models currently available to the user's service account, filters out
  unsuitable specialist models, suggests a balanced current model, and separately checks recipe
  and image compatibility.
- Gemini setup now explains the data-use difference between unpaid and paid service projects and
  links to Google's current terms.
- Recipe imports normalize more provider response variations, ingredient ranges, dual-unit source
  lines, and structured output differences across Gemini and Claude.
- The signed-in home page now shows recently viewed recipes, making a recipe opened earlier easy to
  recover later from another device. Newly updated recipes remain eligible until viewing history is
  established.
- Inbox selection now follows a compact mail-style toolbar. The Trash action appears only after a
  row is selected, and permanent cleanup is protected by an explicit confirmation dialog in the
  Trash view.

## Tester Guidance

- Remove a test AI connection and configure Gemini, OpenAI, or Claude from the new guided screen.
  Confirm model discovery, the suggested model, compatibility results, and provider-specific help.
- With Gemini selected, review the free-tier data-use note and its link before deciding whether an
  unpaid project is appropriate for your recipe material.
- Try Cook Assist with Gemini or Claude. Gemini should use native live voice; Claude voice quality
  depends on the browser and operating system speech facilities.
- Open several recipes, return to Home, and confirm **Recently viewed** reflects the recipes you
  actually visited.
- In Inbox, verify that no Trash action appears until a row is selected. Move an item to Trash,
  restore it, then exercise the separate permanent-empty confirmation with disposable test work.

## Release Identity

The product version is `0.9.0-beta.22` and the source tag is `v0.9.0-beta.22`. The About and Help
interfaces show the exact 12-character source revision used by the production build.

## Compatibility

This release adds one nullable, indexed per-user recipe-view timestamp. Existing recipe state,
ratings, private notes, imports, images, cookbooks, archives, authentication methods, AI
connections, and beta entitlements remain compatible. Provider credentials remain encrypted and
are not rewritten by the expanded provider support or guided setup.
