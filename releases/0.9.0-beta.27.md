# Viandly 0.9.0-beta.27

Released: September 4, 2026

This beta update lets you tailor shopping-list entries for the trip you are planning without
changing the ingredients stored in your recipes.

## Highlights

- Active shopping-list entries now have a pencil control for editing their displayed text in
  place.
- Amounts, package sizes, preferred brands, and other purchasing details can be added directly to
  a shopping entry.
- Compact Save and Cancel controls work across desktop and mobile layouts.
- Editing a combined entry retains all of the recipes that contributed to that shopping row.
- Shopping-list edits never modify source recipes or ingredient data.
- Account exports preserve customized shopping-entry wording.
- Shopping Help now explains list-only editing and combined-entry behavior.

## Tester Guidance

- Open an active shopping list and select the pencil beside an item.
- Change an amount, package size, or brand, then save and confirm that the wording remains after a
  reload.
- Cancel another edit and confirm that the previously saved wording remains.
- Edit an item with multiple source recipes and confirm that all recipe links remain available.
- Open a source recipe and confirm that its ingredient was not changed.

## Release Identity

The product version is `0.9.0-beta.27` and the source tag is `v0.9.0-beta.27`. The About and Help
interfaces show the exact 12-character source revision used by the production build.

## Compatibility

Existing shopping lists, items, recipe sources, store assignments, and handled state are
preserved. Existing entries begin without custom wording. Older Viandly archives remain
importable, and new archives include customized shopping-entry wording.
