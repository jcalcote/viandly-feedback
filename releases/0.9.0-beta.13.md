# Viandly 0.9.0-beta.13

Released: July 25, 2026

This beta update improves recipe duplicate detection and adds a separate way to discover related
recipes while reviewing an import.

## Highlights

- Duplicate warnings now require matching recipe titles in addition to matching source information
  or structured recipe content.
- Independent recipes from the same scanned page, magazine, cookbook, or publisher website are no
  longer treated as duplicates solely because they share attribution.
- Import review can now show up to three **Similar recipes in your library**.
- Each suggestion explains the shared title terms or ingredients and links to the saved recipe for
  comparison.
- Common pantry ingredients such as salt, water, pepper, and oil do not create noisy similarity
  suggestions by themselves.
- Similar-recipe suggestions are informational. They do not affect draft quality, block saving, or
  require a duplicate override.
- Recipe quality diagnostics and Help content now use and explain the same distinction between
  duplicates and related recipes.

## What To Test

Import two different recipes from the same scanned magazine or cookbook page. After saving the
first recipe, confirm that the second draft does not show a duplicate warning merely because both
recipes have the same publication URL.

When a genuinely related dish already exists in your library, confirm that the
**Similar recipes in your library** panel explains the relationship and lets you open the saved
recipe for comparison without interrupting the normal save workflow.

An intentional duplicate with the same title and matching source or recipe content should still
show the existing duplicate warning and **Save duplicate anyway** option.

## Compatibility

Existing recipes and import drafts remain compatible. This release does not require a database
migration.
