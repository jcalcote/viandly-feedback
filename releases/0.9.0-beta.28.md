# Viandly 0.9.0-beta.28

Released: October 1, 2026

Shopping now has separate workflows for in-person trips and orders placed on a vendor's website.

## Highlights

- In-person lists group items by store. Arrange your usual route, override the order for one list,
  move items between stores, and collapse store sections while shopping.
- Staples remember ingredients you commonly keep on hand, including aliases and usual purchase
  preferences. Review recipe ingredients before adding them to a shopping list.
- Online orders are vendor-specific drafts. Add products from your personal catalog, choose a
  package and count, and record what you actually placed with the vendor. Placed orders are
  read-only; Viandly does not track delivery.
- Vendor catalogs remember products, package descriptions, optional item numbers, and product
  links. Vendors with an Express Order URL can use copied item numbers for checkout.
- Shopping Help covers both workflows, store management, staples, and vendor catalogs.

## Tester Guidance

- Check that existing shopping lists still contain their items, recipe sources, store assignments,
  and purchased state.
- Make an in-person list with recipe ingredients and manual items. Try moving items between stores
  and checking them off.
- Create a staple and review how it appears when adding ingredients from a recipe.
- Prepare an online order for one vendor, add a saved or new product and package, then record the
  order as placed. Check that the catalog remembers the products without automatically filling the
  next order.
- Report confusing navigation or unexpected behavior in the [feedback tracker](https://github.com/jcalcote/viandly-feedback/issues).

## Release Identity

The product version is `0.9.0-beta.28` and the source tag is `v0.9.0-beta.28`. The About and Help
interfaces show the exact source revision used by the production build.

## Compatibility

Existing shopping lists remain in-person lists. Their items, recipe sources, store assignments,
and purchased state are preserved. Online orders are separate from recipe-based shopping lists.
Older Viandly archives remain importable, and new archives include the expanded shopping data.
