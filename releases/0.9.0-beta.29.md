# Viandly 0.9.0-beta.29

Released: October 3, 2026

This beta adds shared recipe libraries for trusted households and improves shopping-list entry.

## Highlights

- A library owner with paid or closed-beta sharing access can invite another person from
  **Settings > Recipe libraries**. Invitations require the recipient's verified email address.
- Members can work together on recipes, images, collections, and cookbooks in the owner's library.
  Each person keeps their own favorites, private notes, shopping lists, import inbox, and AI
  connection. The library owner's storage and cookbook access apply to shared content.
- Recipes can be searched across libraries you can access, filtered by library, and saved to a
  chosen library. Owners can manage invitations, members, and ownership transfer.
- Pasted store headings in **Add items** now match a saved in-person store even when punctuation
  differs, including straight and typographic apostrophes. If a heading could mean more than one
  store, Viandly asks for the exact name.
- The **Add recipes** search icon in an in-person shopping list no longer overlaps typed text.

## Try It

- Invite a second Viandly account to your library and confirm it can find and edit a shared recipe
  without gaining access to your personal shopping lists or inbox.
- Paste shopping items beneath a store heading using a different apostrophe style from the saved
  store name. The items should be assigned to that store.
- Open **Add recipes** on a shopping list and check that the search icon and text are separated.
- Report unexpected behavior in the [feedback tracker](https://github.com/jcalcote/viandly-feedback/issues).

## Compatibility

Existing recipes, collections, and cookbooks remain in each account owner's personal library.
This release preserves existing shopping lists and does not change online-order behavior.
